How it works

Seat counts are checked every few minutes. {{ SITE_NAME }} never logs in to Experience and never registers for you.

  1. Find the course

    Search by code, title or instructor to see every section's live seats, waitlist, time and instructor.

  2. Use the waitlist, or get in line

    If a section has an open official waitlist, we send you to join it in Experience. If it doesn't, or after waitlists close for add/drop, flip the switch and we'll email you the CRN when a seat opens.

  3. Or trade

    In 007 but want 011? When someone in 011 wants 007, we introduce you both so you can swap.
