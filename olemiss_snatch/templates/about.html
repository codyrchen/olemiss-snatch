About {{ SITE_NAME }}

How it works

Every few minutes, {{ SITE_NAME }} checks the seat counts in Ole Miss's public class search. When a section you subscribed to goes from full to open, we email you its CRN.

Who gets notified?

Subscribers are lined up in the order they subscribed. When seats open, the first few people in line get an email (three per open seat). Your dashboard shows your place in line.

Does it register me?

No. {{ SITE_NAME }} never logs in to Experience or Banner and never asks for your password. You register yourself in Experience.

What do you store?

Your Ole Miss email and the sections you're watching. That's it.
